HEALTH INFORMATION KNOWLEDGE BASE

GENERAL SAFETY

This knowledge base provides general educational health information.

It is not a substitute for professional medical advice.

Users should speak with qualified healthcare professionals for diagnosis and treatment.

EMERGENCY INFORMATION

Severe chest pain, severe difficulty breathing, loss of consciousness, uncontrolled bleeding, signs of stroke, severe allergic reactions, seizures, and other potentially life-threatening symptoms require immediate professional medical attention.

Do not delay emergency care while waiting for an AI response.

HEALTHY LIFESTYLE

Healthy routines can include adequate sleep, regular physical activity appropriate for the individual, balanced nutrition, hydration, stress management, and regular healthcare visits.

MEDICAL REPORTS

Laboratory reference ranges vary between laboratories.

A result outside a reference range does not automatically mean that a person has a disease.

Medical results should be interpreted together with symptoms, medical history, medications, physical examination, and other relevant information by a healthcare professional.

MEDICATION SAFETY

Medication information should be discussed with a doctor or pharmacist.

Users should not independently start, stop, or change prescription medications based only on AI-generated information.

SYMPTOMS

Symptoms can have many possible causes.

An AI system can provide educational information about possible explanations but cannot reliably diagnose a medical condition from symptoms alone.

PERSONAL HEALTH DATA

Medical information is sensitive.

A production health application should use appropriate authentication, encryption, access controls, consent management, secure storage, and data deletion mechanisms.
